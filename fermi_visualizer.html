What is being pulled?

EF is not a particle. It is the energy at which a state is 50% occupied. Doping changes how many carriers must be explained; EF moves until Fermi–Dirac plus the density of states produce that number.

1 · Density of states N(E)

2 · Fermi–Dirac f(E)

3 · Carriers n(E), p(E)

The three plots share one energy axis. The orange dashed line is EF — identically f(EF) = ½. Shaded areas in plot 3 show shape (DOS × occupancy), not the absolute n0 on the dashboard.

Cause → effect
