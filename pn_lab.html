Shockley I-V

I = I0 (eqVA/kT − 1). The marker is this bias. The exponential is the barrier dropping, not a switch.

Hover symbols in the live readout. Forward: more majority carriers climb a lower barrier. Reverse: that climb is shut; leftover current is mostly minority drift toward −I0.

Bulk Fermi shift

n = ni exp((EF−Ei)/kT). p = ni exp((Ei−EF)/kT). np = ni2. Exponential in the exponent, not a linear slide.

P-side: EF toward Ev, holes up, electrons down. N-side: EF toward Ec, the reverse. The junction then has to connect those two bulk pictures with one electrostatic potential.
