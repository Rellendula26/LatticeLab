Part A · Si atomic density

Conservation reminder

A photon is nearly vertical in this plot: large ΔE, tiny Δk. A phonon is nearly horizontal: small ΔE, large Δk. Silicon’s conduction-band minimum is near X, so both are required.

These E(k) curves are teaching parabolas, not DFT. Heights match the usual textbook gaps (Si 1.12 eV, GaAs 1.42 eV) and the Γ–X offset.

Electron sits at the valence-band maximum (k = 0). Press Excite to conserve energy — and, in Si, crystal momentum.
